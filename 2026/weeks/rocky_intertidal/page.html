🧠🐛❓ Would you like to know more?

Read the classic Connell (1961)

Read the classic Robert T. Paine (1966)! Or, the extended remix of R. T. Paine (1974). Want to hear more of the story with respect fo facilitation? See Lafferty Kevin D. et al. (2016)

Read the classic Lubchenco (1978) on how snails shape biodiversity here in New England rockpools!

For one of the best reviews of rocky shores as the fruit-fly of experimental ecology, see Hawkins et al. (2020)

How do we put together a big theoretical understanding of the rocky intertidal? See Menge and Sutherland (1976) - and if you want that modified by thoughts about facilitation, see Bruno, Stachowicz, and Bertness (2003)

Want an experimental test of the facilitation done by canopies in the rocky intertidal? See Bertness et al. (1999)

What about an alga creating a whole community unto itself? See Bracken, Gonzalez-Dorantes, and Stachowicz (2007)

Would you like a broad biogeographic view of the rocky intertidal of the Gulf of Maine? See Bryson, Trussell, and Ewanchuk (2014)
