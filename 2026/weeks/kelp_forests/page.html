🗓️ Week 05 - Kelp Forests and the Rocky Temperate Subtidal

🌊 🦀 Tides for this week

In this week, we will focus on kelp forests as a model system for understanding marine ecology. We will look at the role of trophic control versus environmental control (top-down versus bottom-up) in this key marine ecosystem. We will discuss the role of foundation species as opposed to keystone species. We will also examine the importance of kelp forests to the ecology and ecosystem services of New England.

🧠🐛❓ Would you like to know more?

For the classic reference on otters and kelp, see Estes and Palmisano (1974). If you want a deeper dive, listen to Jim Estes himself talk about his story in part 1 of kelp worlds from one of my favorite podcasts, Future Ecologies - it’s actually worth listening to all three parts of kelp worlds.

For more on variation in the otter-urchin-kelp paradigm, see .

For the paper that really shaped the paradigm of the cycles between barrens and urchins along with their relationship to food scarcity and waves, see Harrold and Reed (1985)

For the only global data-driven analysis of kelp forest trends through time, see Krumhansl et al. (2016).

For a great review of the kelp-turf ongoing transformation, see Filbee-Dexter and Wernberg (2018)
