🗓️ Week 12 - Urban Marine Ecology

🌊 🦀 Tides for this week

This week we will discuss how cities alter the rules of the game for ecological interactions. How are urban ecosystems in the ocean different than non-urban areas or different than terrestrial urban areas. This will involve thinking about the history of Boston, the unique environments of cities, and how eco-engineering is attempting to level the playing field in urban ecosystems.
